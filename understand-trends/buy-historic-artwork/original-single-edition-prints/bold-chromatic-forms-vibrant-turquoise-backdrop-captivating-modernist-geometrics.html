“Camping that one summer.”

Captivating modernist geometrics come alive on the canvas, inviting the viewer to step into a visionary world of bold, chromatic forms. Intersecting lines, arches, and planes converge in a harmonious symphony, echoing the avant-garde spirit that defined this artistic movement. Warm hues like amber and ochre radiate against the vibrant turquoise backdrop, creating a sense of dynamic tension and forward momentum.
