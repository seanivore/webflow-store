Driving into Downtown

Kaleidoscopic fractals distort reality, glitching cityscapes into Tron-like digital dreamscapes. Towering skyscrapers flicker and pixelate, their rigid geometric forms dissolving into prismatic noise. The urban sprawl warps and fragments, mirroring our collective techno-anxiety about technology's exponential growth outpacing humanity's grasp. Amidst the digital detritus, the boundaries between natural and artificial blur, the organic succumbing to the viral spread of ones and zeroes. This glitched world questions our metaphysical relationship with the cold mechanics underpinning our hyper-connected existence. Yet, the mesmeric disintegration of form into iridescent abstractions betrays an underlying, almost spiritual longing for digital transcendence.
