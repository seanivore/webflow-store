Trustworthy but Mysterious

Smoldering glances drift across half-lit curtain-filtered rooms. A smoke-wreathed bombshell sips from a crystal tumbler, the ice cubes clinking as her gloved fingers toy with the stem. Enveloping shadows accentuate sculpted cheekbones and a ruby pout, a single tear threatens to streak her perfectly contoured face.
