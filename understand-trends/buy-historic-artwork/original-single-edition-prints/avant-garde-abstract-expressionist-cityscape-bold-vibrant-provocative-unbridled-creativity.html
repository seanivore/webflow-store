“Blame it on the mountain.”

Bold brush strokes create an abstract cityscape frozen in dynamic motion — towering forms twist and bend into one another amidst a vibrant whirlwind of color. Daring hues clash and meld in rhythmic harmony, evoking the raw energy and audacious spirit of the avant-garde. Jagged planes and fractured edges slice through the canvas, simultaneously orderly and chaotic, challenging perception through their angular geometry. This utopian vision, both futuristic and nostalgic, invites the viewer to reimagine the urban landscape as a daring exploration of form, texture, and emotion. The composition's unrestrained expressionism captures the pioneering essence of abstract expressionism, where paint becomes a conduit for pure artistic expression, unbound by convention.
