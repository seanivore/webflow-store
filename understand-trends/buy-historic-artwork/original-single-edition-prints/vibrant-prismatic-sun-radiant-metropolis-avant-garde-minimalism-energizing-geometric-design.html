“Waves of Fog.”

Vibrant hues burst forth from a kaleidoscopic sun, its prismatic rays radiating across the skyline in an exuberant interplay of light and form. Angular towers thrust skyward, their sleek facades a harmonious cacophony of geometric shapes that echo the audacious spirit of modernity.
