“Didn’t make quarterly goals.”

The image portrays a striking surrealist composition, blending the human form with fragmented film strips in a dreamlike manner. The figure's stark, Cubist-inspired features appear obscured, hinting at the mysterious and unsettling nature of the scene. The spiraling, disjointed film strips lend a sense of movement and disorientation, inviting the viewer to ponder the subconscious narratives and symbolic meanings woven throughout this enigmatic visual.
