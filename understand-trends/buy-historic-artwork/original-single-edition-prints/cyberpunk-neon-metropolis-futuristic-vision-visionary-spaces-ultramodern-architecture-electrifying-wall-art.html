Weathered Dystopia

Steel monoliths blaze with neon intensity against a cyberpunk skyline. Tinted clouds part to reveal towering edifices that loom, half-obscured in moody haze - urban behemoths encased in glowing circuitry. The megalopolis radiates with electric luminescence, its soaring skyscrapers echoing an audacious future-noir aesthetic.
