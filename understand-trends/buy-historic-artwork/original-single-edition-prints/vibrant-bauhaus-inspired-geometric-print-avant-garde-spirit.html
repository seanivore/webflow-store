“Under-appreciated.”

Shapes interlock and overlap, sharp angles and bold colors colliding in a geometric symphony. Black circles and stripes punctuate the vibrant hues, creating a sense of dynamism and movement. Lines and planes intersect, converging in an abstract exploration of form and line. The composition is at once harmonious and discordant, a visual representation of the pioneering spirit that defined the avant-garde movements of the early 20th century.
