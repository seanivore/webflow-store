“Take my photo in front of this street art.”

This dynamic composition evokes the bold, geometric aesthetic characteristic of the Cubist movement. Fragmented, angular shapes interlock and overlap in a vibrant, harmonious palette of greens, grays, blacks and warm ochre tones. The central leaf-like form commands attention, its stylized planes and facets seemingly in motion against the surrounding abstract planes and angular configurations. This geometric abstraction captures the Cubists' revolutionary approach, deconstructing natural forms into their essential geometric components. The result is a captivating visual experience that challenges the viewer to perceive the world through a new, innovative artistic lens.
