“Mirrors at magic hour.”

This bold, geometric cityscape captures the vibrant, avant-garde spirit of the Constructivist movement. Towering monoliths in saturated hues of gold, ochre, and indigo rise up from the canvas, their sharp angles and clean lines conveying a sense of dynamic, industrial energy. The buildings appear to pulsate with a rhythmic heartbeat, their contrasting colors and intersecting forms emblematic of the Constructivists' utopian vision for an idealized, harmonious society. Grounded in principles of functionality and minimalism, yet imbued with a pioneering, modernist sensibility, this captivating work invites the viewer to step into an abstract, visionary world of architectural dynamism and bold, visionary optimism.
