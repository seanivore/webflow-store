Vogue

Vivid cerulean waves crest in stylized ripples encompassing a serene yet surreal bust, an ethereal meld of human and nature. Branches sprout organically from the figure's verdant visage, bursting with vibrant flora and petaled foliage that intermingles with windswept tresses.
