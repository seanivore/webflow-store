“Peering down from the 44th floor.”

Fractured planes collide in a kaleidoscope of geometric abstraction, their stark angles and intersecting lines defying conventional perspective. A riot of bold stripes and speckled dots converge in a symphony of industrial textures, evoking the grit and grime of urban landscapes. Warm saffron hues bleed into cool teal expanses, their contrasts amplified by the jagged contours that slice through the canvas. This postmodern symphony of shapes and shades resonates with a raw, unfiltered energy, simultaneously jarring and hypnotic, echoing the resilience of the human spirit amidst the relentless march of progress.
