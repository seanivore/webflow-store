Temporary Portal

Pulsating neon hues streak across the glass panes, refracting light into prismatic rivers that cascade over monolithic edifices. The glitching, cyberpunk dystopia thrums with an undercurrent of raw, technological energy, its skyscraper monoliths both awe-inspiring and unsettling. Order and chaos intertwine in a kinetic danceacroof fragmented perspectives, as if reality itself is fraying at the seams, pulled taut by the city's thrumming lifeforce.
