“Visiting the city with no color.”

Stern monoliths pierce the horizon, stretching towards an ashen sky. Sharp, angular forms converge in jagged unity, embodying an industrial rawness that borders on the futuristic. A symphony of geometric shapes echoes through the metallic landscape, their harsh lines softened by subtle gradients of silver and obsidian.
