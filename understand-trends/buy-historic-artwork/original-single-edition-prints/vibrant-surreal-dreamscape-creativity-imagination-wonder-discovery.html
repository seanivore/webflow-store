Three Bodies Problem

Vivid, swirling shapes and vibrant hues give way to a surreal dreamscape, where the boundaries between reality and fantasy blur. A mesmerizing vortex of colors and forms beckons, drawing the viewer into a realm where the impossible becomes possible.
