“Falling into you.”

Kaleidoscopic shapes and vibrant hues swirl in a dizzying display, evoking the bold, experimental spirit of the Fauvists. Unrestrained brushstrokes dance across the canvas, unleashing a primal energy that overwhelms the senses.
