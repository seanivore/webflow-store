Loves Commitment but Is Clingy

Intrigue veils the shadowy silhouette, whose fedora casts a hardboiled mystique. Cigarette smoke swirls ethereally around the enigmatic figure, his stoic features betraying neither motive nor emotion. The chiaroscuro lighting casts harsh angles across the lone protagonist, a brooding specter navigating the seedy underbelly of a neon-drenched metropolis where deception lurks in every darkened alley.
