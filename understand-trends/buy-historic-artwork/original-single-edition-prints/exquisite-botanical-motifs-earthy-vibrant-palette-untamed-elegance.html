Fluid Tendrils — Letter ‘L’

Organic tendrils unfurl and swirl in lush shades of coral and amber, entwining with feathery fronds of sage and slate. Whimsical arabesques and sinuous lines evoke a sense of fluidity and movement, as if the botanical motifs are dancing across the canvas.
