“She left the light on for me.”

Vibrant shards of saturated color converge and fracture, radiating outward like shockwaves from an unseen detonation. Bold geometries clash and interlock, their sharp angles echoing the urban sprawl that birthed this defiant artistic revolution. Depths yawn between towering slabs of pigment, inviting the eye to plunge into surreal chasms where light and shadow intermingle. This kinetic symphony of shape and hue captures the raw, anarchic energy of abstract expressionism, dismantling traditional perspectives in favor of visceral emotion writ large upon the canvas. Immersive and unsettling, it dares the viewer to abandon logic and surrender to the subconscious currents that power true creative liberation.
