“Last summer.”

Step into a vibrant, sun-kissed world of color and movement. Charming seaside buildings in joyful hues of yellow, orange, and teal line the bustling harbor, their rooftops alive with the whimsical dance of seabirds in flight. Boats of every size and shape float upon the shimmering azure waters, their reflections mirroring the lively scene above. The air is thick with the scent of fresh seafood and the laughter of local townspeople browsing the lively marketplace stalls. This is a place of boundless optimism and energy, where the Fauvist palette and expressive brushstrokes capture the very essence of a coastal town alive with the promise of endless summer. Immerse yourself in this captivating vision, where the boundaries between land, sea, and sky blur, inviting you to lose yourself in a world of pure color and joie de vivre.
